Welcome

Let's get Lidarr YouTube Downloader set up.

1 · Connect to Lidarr

Check that the app can reach your Lidarr instance.

Lidarr's URL and API key are configured through environment variables — LIDARR_URL and LIDARR_API_KEY. Set them in your docker-compose.yml / .env and restart the container, then test again.

2 · Folders

Where downloads are written, and where your Lidarr music library lives.

These must be the same paths Lidarr sees (matching your volume mounts), so imports land in the right place. You can change them later in Settings.

3 · All set

Save your folders and jump into the dashboard. Everything else can be tuned in Settings.
